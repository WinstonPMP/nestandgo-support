Privacy Policy — Nest & Go!

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy describes how the iOS and iPadOS version of Nest & Go! handles information.

Game progress and settings

You can play without creating an account. The game saves your progress, in-game balances, and preferences on your device. You can remove this local data by deleting the app from your device.

Unity engine statistics

The game is built with Unity. The Unity engine may send technical information such as the device model, operating system, and hardware capabilities to Unity for engine statistics and compatibility. See Unity’s Game Player and App User Privacy Policy for details about its processing and privacy choices. The current release has gameplay analytics and advertising disabled.

Apple services

Apple handles App Store downloads and payment information for purchases made through the App Store. We do not receive payment card details. See Apple’s App Store privacy notice.

Support and privacy requests

If you email us, we use the email address and information you choose to share to respond and resolve your request. We keep the correspondence as needed for that purpose. To ask about this policy or request deletion of your support correspondence, email thanasekar1988apps2@gmail.com.
