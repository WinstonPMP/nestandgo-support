Frequently asked

How do I play? Tap a cart to drive it out of the yard along its arrow — if the lane is clear it parks and picks up critters. Match three identical Nestlings on the bench to merge them into a bigger one.

A level feels stuck. Every level is solvable. Try freeing carts in a different order, or use a booster. Use Undo if you tap the wrong one.

Do I need internet? No — you can play offline.
